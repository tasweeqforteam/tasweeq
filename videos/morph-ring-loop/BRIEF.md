## Intent

A 5-second seamless looping motion piece for an Instagram Reel. Eight identical
deep forest green (#1B4332) shapes sit on a ring centered on a soft mint green
(#B8E6C8) background. The ring rotates clockwise at a constant, linear speed
(half a turn per loop, so one full turn takes about 10s). Each shape also turns
on its own axis and keeps facing along the ring.

## Customizations

Morph sequence, each form blending into the next with power2.inOut:

1. 0.0–0.6s: squares alternating with 45° diamonds, nearly touching, with an 8-point star cut out at the center
2. 0.6–1.2s: organic blobs with a high border-radius, slightly overlapping into a scalloped flower ring
3. 1.2–1.8s: teardrops with the point facing in, tilted clockwise like a pinwheel
4. 1.8–2.5s: smaller leaf / marquise shapes on a wider ring, with gaps between them
5. 2.5–3.2s: semicircles with the flat edge facing in, in a gear-like ring on a slightly tighter radius
6. 3.2–3.8s: rounded triangles (guitar picks) forming a turbine
7. 3.8–4.3s: larger rounded squares on a wide ring, loosely octagonal, with a faint guide circle behind them
8. 4.3–5.0s: inward-pointing arrows packed into a starburst, then back to phase 1

The ring radius breathes: it widens for leaves and squares and tightens for the rosette and arrows.
