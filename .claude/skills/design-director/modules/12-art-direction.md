# 12 · Creative Development, Art Direction & Originality

## 1. Developing directions

After research and before production, write **2–3 genuinely different**
directions. Skip this when the user has already fixed the direction: then
preserve it and refine within it.

Different means a different **idea or composition strategy**, not a recolour.
A good test: if you swapped their headlines, would the designs still work? If
yes, they are not different directions.

```
DIRECTION  Name (2–3 words)
Core idea ............ one sentence
Strategy ............. what it makes the audience think/feel/do
Metaphor ............. (if any)
Art direction ........ light, material, styling, image source
Composition .......... e.g. "product dominant, 60% field, type as a quiet column"
Grid ................. system from module 04
Typography ........... faces + hierarchy model (AR/EN)
Colour ............... base / accent / ratio
Image strategy ....... photo, AI-hybrid, SVG, type-only (module 11 brief)
Brand integration .... logo zone, device, colour ownership
Negative space ....... where and why
Fit .................. why it suits this audience
Risks ................ e.g. "abstract: may underplay the offer", "relies on image generation quality"
```

Recommend one, with a reason tied to the brief and evidence. Present the
alternatives briefly. Do not make the user read three essays.

## 2. Originality control

References teach principles. They are not templates.

**Extract**: compositional logic, typographic approach, storytelling technique,
lighting strategy, colour relationships, the campaign variation rule.

**Do not reproduce**: another creator's composition, unique artwork, signature
device, distinctive brand assets or campaign execution. Changing the colour or
swapping the product still counts as reproduction.

**Similarity check before delivery.** Compare the final against each reference
on five axes: layout skeleton, focal subject and its pose/crop, type treatment,
colour palette, signature device. Matching on **3 or more** axes is too close,
so change at least two (usually the skeleton and the device). Record the check
in the QA notes.

## 3. Art direction controls

Keep a one-page "visual world" spec for campaigns: light (direction, hardness,
temperature), palette with values, materials and textures, styling props
(allowed/forbidden), camera distance and angle range, grade (contrast, black
level, grain), casting/subject rules, and type behaviour on images. Every new
asset is checked against it.
