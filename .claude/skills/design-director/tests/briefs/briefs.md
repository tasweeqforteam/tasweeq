# Test briefs

All brands, events and domains are fictional (`.example` domains are reserved
for examples). Each brief was built through the skill's own workflow. See
`tests/cases/<id>/NOTES.md` for the record.

## A · Arabic advertising poster
**Client:** بُنّ الوادي (specialty coffee roaster) · **Placement:** Instagram feed 1080×1350
**Objective:** first-order offer. **Audience:** Arabic-reading urban coffee drinkers.
**Mandatory copy (exact):** see `cases/A-arabic-poster/copy.json`: brand, two-line headline, sub-line, CTA with ٢٠٪, URL.
**Requirements:** Arabic headline · product imagery · RTL hierarchy · clear focal point · controlled negative space · accurate typography.

## B · English editorial brochure spread
**Client:** *Urban Quarterly* · **Format:** A4 spread at 150 dpi (2480×1754)
**Content:** feature "The Quiet City": kicker, headline, standfirst, byline, ~380-word body with two subheads, pull quote, two figures with captions, running heads, folios.
**Requirements:** multi-column grid · headline/body hierarchy · image–caption relationships · consistent margins · readable body · editorial rhythm.
